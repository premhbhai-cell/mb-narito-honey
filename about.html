About Mb Naturo Pure

Mb Naturo Pure brings you 100% pure and natural honey from the heart of the Sundarban forest. Our honey is rich in nutrition, free from chemicals and full of natural goodness — collected, filtered and packed with care in Basirhat, North 24 Parganas.

OUR LEADERSHIP

Mahabur Baidy

CEO & Founder, Mb Naturo Pure

Mb Naturo Pure was built around a commitment to bring the goodness of nature closer to everyday homes. As CEO and Founder, Mahabur Baidy guides the brand's focus on natural sourcing, careful handling and customer trust.

Inspired by the rich natural landscape of the Sundarban, we aim to share quality honey while respecting the people, traditions and environment connected with its journey.

From sourcing and quality checks to hygienic packing and customer support, our goal is to make every interaction with Mb Naturo Pure clear, thoughtful and dependable.

Our Mission

To bring chemical-free, pure forest honey to every home at a fair price.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted name in natural honey from the Sundarban.

Our Promise

No added sugar, no shortcuts. What nature gives is what you get.
